# Q16 — Email is a parallel and supporting channel

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Decided:** 2026-09-20/22 (rounds 1–3)
- **Followed interviewer recommendation:** yes

## Question

What is email's role?

## Decision

A parallel channel where the business prefers email, and a supporting channel (confirmations, chasers). Not an independent capability.

## Alternatives considered

- Phone only
- Email as a separate product

## Why

Some businesses prefer email, and written confirmation needs it anyway.

## Consequences

Channel resolution: Q29.
